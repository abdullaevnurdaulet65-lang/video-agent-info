# Privacy Policy - video-agent

video-agent is a personal tool. It creates short videos and uploads them to the YouTube channels of its owner, using the YouTube upload permission that the owner granted.

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. The only credential used is the owner's own OAuth token, which is kept in private GitHub repository secrets and used only to upload videos.
